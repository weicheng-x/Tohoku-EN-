🍁 Nikko 2-Day Autumn Foliage & Tobu Bus Masterplan (World Heritage & Oku-Nikko)

Recommended 2-day bus & walking itinerary from Koga Station ↔ JR Nikko / Tobu Nikko Station for Day 16 (Thu, Nov 5) and Day 17 (Fri, Nov 6)—timed for the early-November peak of Rinnoji Shoyoen Garden, Nikko Toshogu Shrine, and the Irohazaka Winding Road.

Day 16 (Thu, Nov 5) Recommended Route

UNESCO World Heritage Shrines & Rinnoji Shoyoen Garden Foliage Walk

  • 09:30 Arrive at JR Nikko / Tobu Nikko Station: Grab a hot Fried Yuba Manju (sweet bean bun in crispy tofu skin with rock salt) right outside the station at Nikko Sakaeya.
  • 10:00 Shinkyo Sacred Bridge: Ride the World Heritage Sightseeing Bus (Stop 2B) to "Shinkyo" and photograph the vermilion bridge framed by the autumn foliage of the Daiya River gorge.
  • 11:00 Rinnoji Temple & Shoyoen Japanese Garden: Early November is the exact peak season for the historic Edo-period maple garden at Shoyoen.
  • 12:15 Lunch: Dine on Omelet Rice & Nirvana Cheesecake at Meiji no Yakata (inside the shrine grounds) or yuba (tofu skin) specialties at Hipparidako or Uoyo.
  • 13:30 Nikko Toshogu Shrine, Futarasan Shrine & Taiyuin: Explore the lavishly carved Yomeimon Gate, Sleeping Cat, and forest trails before taking the JR Nikko Line back to Koga.
Day 17 (Fri, Nov 6) Recommended Route

Irohazaka Winding Road, Lake Chuzenji, Kegon Falls & Ryuzu Falls

  • Value Bus Pass: Pick up the Chuzenji Onsen Free Pass (¥2,300) or Yumoto Onsen Free Pass (¥3,500) at the Tobu Nikko Station Tourist Center.
  • 08:30–09:00 Early Departure Rule: Board an early Yumoto Onsen-bound Tobu Bus from JR/Tobu Nikko Station (Stop 2A) to beat autumn traffic up the 48-hairpin Irohazaka Winding Road.
  • 10:00 Chuzenji Onsen Bus Stop (~48 min): Walk 5 minutes to Kegon Falls (take the elevator down to the gorge viewing deck) and stroll along the autumn shores of Lake Chuzenji. Enjoy handmade yuba soba for lunch at lakeside Teuchi Soba Katsura.
  • 13:00 Extend to Oku-Nikko: Ride the bus 15 minutes further to Ryuzu Falls (Dragon Head Waterfall) and Senjogahara Marshland.
    *Note: Because the Akechidaira Ropeway is suspended in 2026, focusing directly on Lake Chuzenji, Kegon Falls, and Ryuzu Falls gives you the smoothest experience.
